Sales Decline & Profit Leakage Investigation

Project Overview

An interactive Business Intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to investigate sales performance, identify profit leakage, and uncover the key factors contributing to financial losses.

Using the Superstore dataset, this project analyzes sales trends, profitability, discount strategies, regional performance, and product categories to generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

Business Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profitability performance.
* Identify the main drivers of profit loss.
* Investigate the impact of discount strategies on profitability.
* Compare sales and profit performance across regions.
* Evaluate product category performance.
* Provide data-driven recommendations to improve profitability.

Tools & Technologies

* Microsoft Power BI: Interactive dashboards and data visualization.
* Power Query: Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
* DAX: Calculated measures and business KPIs.
* Microsoft Excel: Dataset management and data source.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Key Findings

* Uncontrolled discounting was identified as a major contributor to profit leakage.
* Higher discount levels were associated with significant profitability challenges.
* The Central region showed particular vulnerability to high discount rates.
* Profitability varied across product categories and geographical regions.
* Lower discount levels generally resulted in healthier profit performance.

Business Recommendations

* Establish clear discount policies and approval limits.
* Review high-discount transactions to minimize unprofitable sales.
* Monitor regional profitability, particularly in the Central region.
* Evaluate product-level profitability before applying discounts.
* Track profit margins and loss orders regularly.
